HOW IT WORKS

YOU ARE THE TOWER

Nothing is built on the map. Every shot comes from your own cursor and drains the energy cell, so aim beats spam.

THE LINE LEAKS

Creeps walk the trench toward the core. Anything that reaches it tears out hull points. Twenty is all you get.

SPEND THE SCRAP

Kills drop scrap. Between waves turn it into damage, faster recharge, a wider shockwave, or hull repairs.
